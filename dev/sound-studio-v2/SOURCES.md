# Sound Studio V2 — sources and licensing

November 1, 2026. All 27 V2 candidates are original deterministic procedural
sound design, generated by `prepare.mjs`. No external recordings, sampled
instruments, borrowed melody, actor performance, cloned voice or movie audio
were used. Chucky's three laughs are synthesized character vocals made with a
glottal harmonic source, moving vowel formants and breath noise. They do not
imitate a real person or an existing film performance.

These are project originals with no third-party audio attribution obligation.
This is provenance documentation, not a claim of exclusive copyright in
generated material. Jeremy's listening approval is still required before use
in gameplay. The three laugh candidates especially need listening judgment for
recognizability and fatigue; signal checks cannot establish either.

Every WAV has a retained filename, SHA-256, duration, byte count, peak/RMS,
recipe, author and source description in `catalog.json`. Regenerate offline
with `node dev/sound-studio-v2/prepare.mjs`. The global seeded noise sequence
is deterministic at the recorded recipe order. Exports are mono PCM16, 32 kHz,
with DC removal, a 4 ms entrance fade and 45 ms exit fade. No runtime synthesis
or network fetch is required. Playback gain 1.0 is the proposed baked-in level;
the studio's listening slider only attenuates it. Peak/RMS are not LUFS.

24 assets in pass2-catalog.json are generated by prepare-pass2.mjs. The 27 original files are untouched. Cup takes use the retained Kenney Casino Audio dice-shake-1.ogg (CC0 1.0, https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio), randomized overlapping recorded windows, fixed resampling and soft filtering.
